# Privacy Policy — Zone Alert

_Last updated: 2026-06-18_

Zone Alert ("the app," "we") is a heart‑rate training aid. **Your privacy is simple:
everything stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data.**

## What the app processes (all on‑device)
- **Heart‑rate data** from your Bluetooth heart‑rate sensor — used live to show your
  zones and alerts. Processed in memory and stored only on your device.
- **Resting‑HR, aerobic‑threshold, and workout records** — saved locally on your device
  (in the app's local storage) so you can see history and trends.
- **Location (GPS)** — used **only on your device** to calculate workout distance and pace.
  Your location is **never** transmitted off the device or stored as a track. The app
  requests background location so distance keeps recording while your screen is locked.
- **Profile inputs** you enter (age, sex, weight, resting HR, Max HR) — stored locally to
  calculate your zones and calorie estimates.

## What we do NOT do
- No accounts, no sign‑in, no servers, no cloud.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third‑party SDKs.
- We never sell or share your information with anyone.

## Optional: Fitbit
If you choose to connect Fitbit (Fitbit tab), the app talks to **Fitbit's servers** to
log you in (via Apple's secure web sheet) and to fetch *your own* Fitbit data — steps,
calories, resting heart rate, and sleep. Your Fitbit access token is stored only on your
device. We never send your Zone Alert / heart-rate-strap data to Fitbit; the Fitbit data
is kept **separate** from your on-device workout data and is only requested when you
connect or finish a workout. You can disconnect at any time in the Fitbit tab. If you
never connect Fitbit, the app makes no network connections at all.

## Data leaving your device
Apart from the optional Fitbit connection above, the only time information leaves your
device is when **you** explicitly tap **Export PDF report** (or **Export raw test data**)
and choose where to send or save it. That action, and its destination, are entirely under
your control.

## Notifications
Zone alerts are **local notifications** generated on your device. No notification content
is sent to any server.

## Permissions the app asks for, and why
- **Bluetooth** — to connect to your heart‑rate sensor.
- **Location (incl. background)** — to measure distance and pace during a workout.
- **Notifications** — to alert you when you leave your target heart‑rate zone.

You can revoke any of these in iOS Settings at any time; the app continues to work with
reduced functionality.

## Children
Zone Alert is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
