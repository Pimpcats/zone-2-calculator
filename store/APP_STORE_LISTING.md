## Description
Zone Alert turns any Bluetooth heart‑rate strap into a smart zone coach.

Set the heart‑rate range you want to train in, and Zone Alert buzzes and shows a banner the
moment you drop below or climb above it — even with your screen locked or the app in your
pocket. No more glancing at your wrist every 20 seconds.

**Train by heart rate**
• Pick a target zone (or type exact bpm) and get instant alerts when you leave it
• % of Max HR or Heart‑Rate Reserve zone math
• Adapts to you: your resting‑HR test nudges your zones over time

**A clean live dashboard**
• Big heart‑rate readout, current zone, and a scrollable graph of your whole session
• Distance and pace from GPS, calories, and duration
• Time‑in‑zone breakdown

**Test and track**
• 5‑minute resting heart‑rate test
• Max‑HR test with a VO₂ estimate
• Experimental HRV "adaptive threshold" test to find the top of your easy zone
• Daily and weekly progress, plus resting‑HR and threshold trend charts

**Works with your gear**
• Any standard Bluetooth Low Energy heart‑rate sensor (chest straps and armbands)
• Built‑in compatibility scanner tells you if a sensor supports HR and HRV
• Pairs to one strap and ignores everyone else's nearby

**Private by design**
• Everything stays on your device — no account, no servers, no tracking, no ads
• Export a clean PDF of your data whenever you want

Zone Alert is a fitness training aid, not a medical device, and its readings are estimates.
Consult a physician before intense exercise.
